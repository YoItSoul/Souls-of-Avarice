# Souls of Avarice — Gamestage Unlock Guide

Every progression gate in the pack is a **gamestage**. Stages lock recipes, items, ores,
dimensions and mobs until you earn them. This is the cheat-sheet for *how to get each one*
and *what it opens up*.

> **Casual mode note:** in Casual, stages are unlocked automatically as you go — this guide
> matters most in **Adventure** and **Expert**, where the gates are enforced.
> Most stages are earned from the **Main Questline** chapter (shown as *Quest: …*); a few are
> earned purely by crafting and **right-clicking a key item** (shown as *Item:*).

### Notes
- **"Stage recovery" / "Restore Stage" quests** exist for `getting_started`, `novice_tinker` and
  `twilight_forest` — click-to-complete safety nets if a stage ever fails to apply.
- Many milestone items (Ender Charm, Twilight Shield, Bravery Certificate, Awakened Eye, Arcane
  Crystal Ball, Solarium Star, Death Coin) are **consumed on right-click** to grant their stage —
  the quest for each simply asks you to hold one, so craft two if you want to keep it as a trophy.
- Source of truth for grants: `soa_additions/quests/*.json` (`grant_stage` rewards) + the mod's
  `StageItem`s; enforcement lives in `scripts/soa_item_stages.zs`. See
  `tools/audit/stage_grant_parity.md` for the GreedyCraft↔SOA parity audit.
